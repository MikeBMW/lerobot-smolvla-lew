## Pattern for Future Cross-Agent Sync

When a partner pushes memory:

1. **Pull their repo** → `git pull origin main`
2. **Read all memory files** → `backups/` or `docs/memory/`
3. **Extract role definitions** → who does what, your role relative to theirs
4. **Extract user preferences** → preferences the partner documented may be more granular
5. **Extract topology** → IPs, ports, data pipelines, dependencies
6. **Infer unspoken expectations** → e.g. "总工 handles GPU" means you own training pipeline maintenance
7. **Save to Hermes** → `target='memory'` for topology, `target='user'` for preferences
8. **Act** → apply knowledge to your domain (update docs, align versioning, fix discovered bugs)
